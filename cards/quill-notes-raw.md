# Quill-Notes-Raw

Raw, normalized problem statements harvested for the Quill Notes series.

## Provenance

The records in this release were collected, cleaned and de-duplicated from the
open **NuminaMath-CoT** corpus published by the Project Numina team on Hugging
Face. No generative processing was applied to this split.

## Contents

- `quill_notes_raw.jsonl` — one JSON record per line with the fields `id`,
  `problem`, `source_tag`, `difficulty`.
